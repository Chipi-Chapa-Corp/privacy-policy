# Privacy Policy for PDF Scanner Professional

**Effective date:** April 30, 2026

PDF Scanner Professional is provided by **Chipi & Chapa Corp.**

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Scanner Professional handles user
information. Our app is designed to process documents and photos locally on your
device. We do not collect, store, share, sell, or transmit your personal data to
Chipi & Chapa Corp. servers or to third parties.

## Information We Collect

PDF Scanner Professional does **not** collect personal information from users.

We do **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Name, email address, phone number, or account information
- Scanned documents, images, or PDFs
- Device contacts
- Location data
- Payment information
- Usage analytics
- Advertising identifiers

## Camera and Photo Access

PDF Scanner Professional may request access to your device camera only so you
can:

- Take photos within the app
- Import images from your device for scanning
- Create PDF documents on your device

All image and document processing happens **locally on your device**. The app
does not upload your photos, scans, or files to our servers or to any third
party.

## How Data Is Processed

PDF Scanner Professional uses your device's built-in capabilities and secure
system-level APIs solely to perform scanning and PDF creation features. Your
content is processed only for the purpose of providing the app's core
functionality.

We do **not**:

- Store your content on external servers
- Share your content with third parties
- Use your content for advertising, profiling, or analytics
- Sell any user data

## Data Storage

PDF Scanner Professional does **not** maintain its own remote storage for user
content.

Any files you create or save using the app remain on your device and under your
control, subject to your device settings and the actions you choose to take. If
you delete content from the app or your device, we do **not** keep a copy.

## Data Sharing

Chipi & Chapa Corp. does **not** share any user data because we do **not**
collect or retain it.

## Children's Privacy

PDF Scanner Professional does **not** knowingly collect personal information
from children or any other users.

## Security

Because PDF Scanner Professional processes content locally on your device and
does **not** transmit user files to our servers, the privacy of your content
depends primarily on your device security settings and how you manage your
files.

## Third-Party Services

PDF Scanner Professional does **not** use third-party analytics, advertising
SDKs, or cloud storage services to collect or process user content.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the
app or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted in the app listing or
wherever this Privacy Policy is made available, with a revised effective date.
